# Upholstery Supply and Equipment Sourcing Guide: Markham and Canadian Markets

**Report ID:** TRU-KB-20260108-01
**Date:** 2026-01-08
**Objective:** To create a comprehensive guide for Truper's knowledge base on where to purchase upholstery tools, equipment, and supplies, both locally near Markham, Ontario, and online within Canada. The report is organized by supplier type and location to provide a detailed directory for procurement and project needs.

## Executive Summary

This report provides a detailed analysis of the market for upholstery tools, equipment, and supplies accessible to operations based in Markham, Ontario. The findings are segmented into three primary geographical categories: local suppliers within Markham, regional suppliers across the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), and national online suppliers shipping within Canada. The research identifies a robust network of specialized businesses catering to various needs, from custom furniture manufacturing and reupholstery services to the retail of specific components like fabrics, foam, industrial sewing machines, and hand tools.

While general hardware stores in Markham do not feature dedicated upholstery sections, the area and its surroundings host a significant number of specialized service providers and retailers. Markham itself is home to several custom upholstery shops that offer services and may act as a source for certain materials. The broader GTA, particularly Toronto's fashion district and industrial areas in Concord and Scarborough, presents a much deeper and more diverse supplier base. This includes large-scale fabric wholesalers, industrial sewing machine dealers, and specialized foam cutters. For accessibility and a wider selection, numerous Canadian online retailers provide comprehensive catalogs of tools, fabrics, and supplies, with many offering competitive shipping options. This guide serves as a foundational resource for sourcing all necessary components for professional upholstery work.

## Local Suppliers and Services in Markham, Ontario

The immediate vicinity of Markham offers a concentrated group of businesses primarily focused on providing custom upholstery services rather than operating as dedicated retail supply stores. These establishments, however, are valuable resources, possessing deep expertise and potentially offering access to materials and supplies through their service operations. They are ideal partners for custom projects, repairs, and sourcing high-quality fabrics from designer brands they carry.

**Markham Furniture** stands out as a significant local entity, operating as both a manufacturer and retailer since 1998. This family-owned business specializes in Canadian-made, made-to-order furniture, including sofas, sectionals, and chairs. Their manufacturing process, conducted in-house in Markham, utilizes locally-sourced Canadian hardwood for frames and employs an 8-way hand-tied web and coil spring system, indicative of high-end construction. Their team consists of skilled fabric sewers, springers, carpenters, and upholsterers. For material sourcing, Markham Furniture provides access to a premium selection of designer fabrics from notable brands such as Avant-Garde, Maxwell Fabrics, Vision Fabrics, JF Fabrics, and Robert Allen Fabrics. Their direct-to-consumer model and commitment to local manufacturing make them a key local contact for both finished goods and high-quality upholstery materials.

**Harmony Interiors**, located at 90 Esna Park Drive in Markham, offers specialized services in custom upholstery, refinishing, and the creation of new furniture. Their service-oriented approach includes in-home fabric selection consultations and the support of an in-house interior decorator, ensuring clients choose materials suitable for their specific needs. While primarily a service provider, their access to various fabric lines and a five-year guarantee on workmanship highlight their role as a source for quality materials and professional expertise within Markham. They serve a wide area, including the entire Greater Toronto Area, Richmond Hill, and Aurora.

Other established service providers in Markham include **Gordon's Custom Upholstery**, which has been in operation since 1983. This business offers a comprehensive suite of services including refinishing, reupholstery, custom furniture building, and repairs. They boast a selection of thousands of fabric choices and provide value-added services like free pick-up and delivery. Similarly, **The W Upholstering Ltd.** has been serving the residential and commercial needs of the GTA for over 50 years, offering custom work on a variety of furniture pieces, foam replacement, and access to a large selection of fashionable and traditional fabrics. Additional local upholsterers listed in Markham include **Triton Designs** and **M Force Workshop**, contributing to a local ecosystem of skilled craftspeople.

For direct retail of materials, **Bal Trading Limited** is listed as a retailer of upholstery fabrics within Markham, representing one of the few local options focused specifically on material sales rather than integrated services. For those looking to acquire skills, the **Canadian School Of Reupholstery** in nearby Thornhill provides educational courses, representing an important resource for training and professional development in the field.

## Greater Toronto Area (GTA) Supplier Network

The Greater Toronto Area offers a vast and diverse landscape of suppliers for every facet of the upholstery trade. From the dense concentration of fabric stores in Toronto's Fashion District to industrial suppliers in surrounding municipalities, the region is a critical hub for sourcing materials and equipment.

### Fabric and Textile Specialists

The GTA is home to a multitude of fabric suppliers, each with unique specializations. **King Textiles**, located at 161 Spadina Avenue in Toronto, is a cornerstone of the Fashion District, offering an immense selection of fabrics. Their inventory includes natural fibers like cotton, linen, silk, and wool in numerous weaves and finishes. They also stock a wide range of polyester, rayon, and nylon fabrics. Importantly for the upholstery trade, they have dedicated sections for home decor, featuring upholstery fabric, upholstery vinyl, drapery lining, and outdoor fabrics, including the durable Sunbrella brand.

Nearby on Queen Street West, **Queen Textiles Inc.** has been a wholesale and retail provider since 1993. While offering fabrics for all occasions, they have a notable specialization in Chinese silk brocade, printed cottons, and various wools. Their extensive inventory also includes upholstery fabrics, making them another key stop in the downtown core. **Leo's Textiles**, also on Queen Street West, caters to a high-end market, including fashion designers and the film industry, with a focus on eveningwear fabrics like hand-beaded lace, 3D applique lace, and silk embroidery. While specialized, their high-quality materials could be suitable for decorative or high-end accent upholstery projects.

For a different shopping experience, **Len's Mill Stores**, at 66 Orfus Road in North York, operates a large-format store that combines a wide selection of fashion fabrics with a dedicated upholstery section. This department stocks durable fabrics, various types of foam, and batting. They are also a significant resource for outdoor materials, carrying marine vinyl, indoor/outdoor canvas, and outdoor-grade foam. Their acquisition of stock from the former Designer Fabrics has further broadened their collection of upholstery and drapery materials.

Specialty boutiques also play a vital role in the GTA's fabric market. **Maryan's Fabric Ltd** on Yonge Street is a high-end store specializing in imported fabrics for bridal and evening wear, but also carries luxurious silks, cottons, wools, and linens handpicked from European suppliers. Their commitment to sourcing from companies with eco-friendly production methods is a notable attribute. **Riverside Fabrics** is another boutique with a specific focus on luxury, eco-friendly, and sustainable materials, such as TENCEL™ Lyocell, Merino Wool, and Organic Cotton. They are also a unique source for designer deadstock fabrics sourced from local fashion designers and film studios, which can provide access to unique, high-quality materials at a good value.

In the northern part of the GTA, Concord is home to several fabric wholesalers and retailers, including **NDF Fabrics Ltd**, **Elite Textiles**, and **Fabric Factory Outlet**, all providing options for upholstery fabrics.

### Foam, Tools, and General Supplies

Beyond fabrics, sourcing foundational materials like foam and the requisite tools is crucial. Several GTA businesses specialize in these components. **JT's Outdoor Fabrics**, located in Barrie, which is within the broader GTA region, is a key Canadian supplier with both a physical storefront and a robust online presence. They offer a comprehensive range of professional-grade upholstery tools, including products from the well-regarded manufacturer C.S. Osborne. Their tool selection covers cutting tools like scissors and hot knives, fastener and grommet installation tools, and general hand tools such as seam tools, staple removers, and staplers. They also supply a variety of foam and padding suitable for upholstery, including open-cell foam, closed-cell padding, and specialized outdoor cushion foam that is fast-drying and mildew-resistant.

In North York, **Vitafoam Products Canada Ltd.** is a specialist in foam products, and **Dominion Felt Co.** is another supplier of general upholstery materials. In Pickering, **Triden Distributors** serves as a supplier of components, fabrics, vinyl, foams, and leather to industrial clients across Ontario. For those closer to the eastern part of the GTA, **Ace Upholstery Supplies** in Oshawa is a dedicated source for upholstery supplies and foam products.

While dedicated hardware stores in Markham were found not to have specific upholstery sections, the broader search for tool suppliers reveals that specialized upholstery suppliers are the primary source. The tools required range from hand tools like hog ring pliers, webbing stretchers, and tack claws to fastening equipment like industrial staple guns and button machines. The brand **C.S. Osborne & Co.** is a frequently mentioned manufacturer of high-quality, specialized hand tools for the trade, and their products are available through various distributors, including the aforementioned JT's Outdoor Fabrics.

### Industrial Sewing and Upholstery Equipment

For professional-grade equipment, several dealers in the GTA specialize in industrial sewing machines. **WorldSewCentre**, located in Scarborough, is a family-owned business with over 30 years of experience. They sell and service industrial machines, including models from JUKI, a leading brand in the industry. Their 8,000-square-foot facility also stocks a wide array of parts, fabrics, and notions, making them a one-stop shop.

**International Sewing Machine Group**, with its headquarters in Brampton, is a major Canadian distributor of industrial sewing equipment and parts. They represent a wide range of top-tier brands, including Juki, Consew, Pfaff, Brother, and Durkopp Adler, catering to industries from apparel to upholstery and leather goods. They offer both new and used equipment, providing options for different budget levels.

In Toronto, **General Industrial Sewing Machine Co.** on Champagne Drive is one of Canada's oldest and largest dealers, serving both large industrial clients and smaller alteration or dry-cleaning operations. For sales and service, **Toronto Sewing Company** in Etobicoke and **York Sewing Machines** in East York both offer sales and repair services for industrial and domestic machines, along with parts, notions, and sharpening services. These dealers are essential for acquiring and maintaining the heavy-duty machinery required for professional upholstery work.

## Online Canadian Supplier Directory

For convenience, broad selection, and competitive pricing, online suppliers are an indispensable resource. A number of Canadian-based or Canadian-serving e-commerce stores provide comprehensive access to upholstery tools, equipment, and materials, shipping directly to Markham.

### Online Fabric and Notion Retailers

**Blackbird Fabrics**, an online store based in Vancouver, has established itself as a premier source for high-quality, modern garment fabrics, but its extensive collection of notions and supplies is highly relevant to the upholstery trade. They offer a wide array of buttons, elastics, interfacing, needles, heavy-duty thread, zippers, and various sewing tools. They ship worldwide and offer free shipping on Canadian orders over a certain threshold, making them an accessible source for high-quality ancillary supplies.

**Fabricville** claims to be Canada's largest online fabric store, offering a vast inventory that includes upholstery-relevant materials like vinyl, leather-look fabrics, and a wide range of wovens. They operate physical stores across the country and provide a robust online shopping experience. **Funky Monkey Fabrics**, based in Ontario, is another online retailer known for quality fabrics, including durable options like minky and various knits, and ships from its local warehouse.

For those seeking modern and quilting fabrics that can be used for certain decorative upholstery projects, **Thread Count Fabrics** in Manitoba and **Keystone Modern Creative** in Winnipeg are excellent online sources. They offer curated collections from top designers and manufacturers, with clear shipping policies for Canadian customers, including flat-rate and free shipping options.

### Specialized Online Foam Suppliers

Sourcing the correct type and size of foam is a critical and often challenging aspect of upholstery. Several online suppliers specialize in this area. **Foam Factory, Inc.**, operating online as canada.foambymail.com, ships a vast selection of foam products to Canada. They provide an intuitive online ordering system for custom-cut foam in various shapes and sizes. Their product line includes multiple foam types suitable for upholstery, such as Poly Foam for occasional use, HD36 Foam for regular-use couch cushions, and Lux Foam for firm seating. Critically, they offer **Dryfast Foam**, an open-cell, antimicrobial, and mildew-resistant foam specifically designed for outdoor furniture that allows water to drain through. They offer free shipping to most Canadian locations on qualifying orders.

**JT's Outdoor Fabrics**, in addition to its tools and fabrics, is also an online source for various types of foam and padding. Their selection includes open-cell foam for cushions, dense closed-cell padding for support, and cushion wrapping materials like Terylene (Dacron) and silk film, which are used to add loft and ease the process of inserting foam into covers.

### Tools, Hardware, and General Supplies Online

Large online marketplaces are a major source for tools and supplies. **Amazon.ca** offers a vast and varied selection of upholstery tools from numerous sellers. This includes a wide array of staple guns, from light-duty manual models for small projects to heavy-duty electric and pneumatic staplers for professional use. Brands like Arrow, meite, and NEU MASTER are readily available, often in kits that include staples and staple removers. Beyond staplers, Amazon is a source for hand tools, needles, thread, webbing, and other essential supplies.

**Richelieu Hardware** is a major B2B and B2C supplier with a strong online presence in Canada. Their catalog includes a specific category for upholstery tools and repair supplies, featuring items like tack lifters, upholstery regulators, zippers, grommets, and button shells. They are a reliable source for the small but essential hardware components of upholstery.

For specialized tools, **Upholster.com** is a US-based retailer that ships to Canada, offering a wide range of professional tools, including a large selection from C.S. Osborne. They also sell foam cutters and upholstery-grade sewing machines. While shipping from the US incurs additional costs, their specialized inventory can be invaluable for sourcing specific or hard-to-find tools. Similarly, **DIY Upholstery Supply** is another online resource that stocks a variety of supplies and tools and notes specific products that are stocked in Canada for easier access.

## Conclusion

The sourcing landscape for upholstery supplies, tools, and equipment for a business based in Markham, Ontario, is both rich and multifaceted. While Markham itself has a limited number of direct retail suppliers, it is home to a core group of skilled upholstery service providers who can be valuable partners and sources for high-quality materials. The true depth of the market becomes apparent when expanding the scope to the Greater Toronto Area, which functions as a major commercial hub for the trade. The region offers everything from high-end fabric boutiques and large-scale wholesalers in Toronto to specialized industrial machine dealers and foam suppliers in surrounding municipalities like Scarborough, Concord, and Barrie.

Furthermore, the rise of e-commerce has made sourcing more convenient and competitive than ever. A strong network of Canadian online retailers provides comprehensive access to a global marketplace of fabrics, a wide array of specialized tools from respected brands like C.S. Osborne, and custom-cut foam solutions delivered directly to the workshop. By leveraging a hybrid strategy that combines local relationships with regional and national suppliers, Truper can effectively and efficiently procure all necessary materials and equipment to support its upholstery-related projects, ensuring access to both quality and variety.
